# fldigi decode of a third-party real SITOR-B sample (decoder validation)

Source : https://sigidwiki.com/wiki/SITOR-B  (Signal Identification Wiki, audio sample
         "SITOR-B.mp3": https://www.sigidwiki.com/images/9/96/SITOR-B.mp3)
Content: Mondolfo Radio (NAVAREA III) coastal warning UA69, 13 Jan 2012 — firing exercise,
         southern Adriatic, 7-point polygon. Real off-air recording (not synthetic, not repo-made).
Rig    : headless fldigi 4.2.06, NAVTEX modem, carrier 1000 Hz (default), 12 kHz mono
         (ffmpeg mp3 -> wav). `make decode FILE=data/audio/sitorb/samples/SITOR-B.wav`.
Result : chars=146, star_rate 0.0 %, frames=none (recording starts mid-transmission, so the
         ZCZC header/phasing was not captured; the message body decoded verbatim).

Decoded (verbatim, tail of the transmission):
---
QI WP TIE
E) 40 25 55N - 18 22 28E
F) 40 23 05N - 18 23 18E
G) 40 23 54N - 18 17 30E.
SAILING, ANCHORAGE AND FISHING ARE PROHIBITED.
NNNN

RYRYRYRYRYRYRYRYRYRYRYRYRYRYRYRYRYRYRYRYRY
---

This is a third independent confirmation that the headless fldigi rig decodes real SITOR-B
audio (alongside the 2018 NL Coastguard off-air decode @0 % CER and the gen_sitorb round-trip).
The garbled "QI WP TIE" prefix is the pre-phasing lead-in; a full off-air capture (KiwiSDR U-slot)
includes the ZCZC header.
